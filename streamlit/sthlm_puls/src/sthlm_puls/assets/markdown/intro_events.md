# 🎭 Stockholm Cultural Guide

Discover the best of Stockholm's cultural scene — concerts, theatre, art exhibitions, and more.

This guide helps you find events happening in Stockholm, plan your visit around the weather,
and make the most of what the city has to offer.

---

## What you can explore

- 📅 **This week** — see what's on and find the best day to go out
- 🌦️ **Weather & events** — plan smarter with a 7-day cultural forecast
- 🎵 **Browse by genre, venue or date** — filter events by music, theatre, sports, and more

---

## About the data

Stockholm has a vibrant and diverse cultural scene with hundreds of events every month.
We've gathered data from four of the city's biggest event sources to give you the most
complete picture possible.

Our weather forecast updates automatically every hour using real-time data from Open-Meteo,
so you always know what to expect before heading out.
